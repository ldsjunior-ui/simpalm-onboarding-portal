How to send via Gmail:
  1. Fill in the fields above
  2. Click Copy Email to Clipboard
  3. Open Gmail → Compose new email
  4. Click inside the message body area
  5. Windows: Ctrl+V  |  Mac: Cmd+V
  6. Add recipient & subject → Send ✅
How to send via Gmail (Offer Letter):
  1. Fill in all fields above
  2. Click Generate & Download Offer Letter — save the PDF
  3. Click Copy Email to Clipboard
  4. Open Gmail → Compose new email
  5. Click the message body → Cmd+V / Ctrl+V
  6. Attach the Offer Letter PDF + Personnel Acknowledgement Form
  7. Add recipient & subject → Send ✅
⚠️ Windows users: use Chrome or Edge. If a clipboard permission popup appears, click Allow.
